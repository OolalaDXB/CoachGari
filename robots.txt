# Coach Gari — crawling rules.
#
# Disallowed below are the paths that must never be fetched by anyone: the
# back-office, and the two tokenised routes (/r/<token> a client's report,
# /c/<token> a collaboration room). Those carry a secret in the URL, are served
# no-store, and are never linked from a public page.
#
# Deliberately NOT disallowed: /routes/a, /routes/b, /collab and /p/. They each
# send "noindex" in the page, and a crawler has to be allowed to fetch a page to
# read that. Blocking them here would do the opposite of what it looks like —
# Google can list a URL it was forbidden to open, having never seen the noindex.

User-agent: *
Allow: /
Disallow: /admin
Disallow: /r/
Disallow: /c/

Sitemap: https://coachgari28.com/sitemap.xml
